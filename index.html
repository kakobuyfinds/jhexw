What to check before ordering through SuperBuy

Find the original listing

Open the HipobuyFind item page and look for the original marketplace URL. Paste that URL into SuperBuy to check whether your color and size are available. Match the measurements against shoes or clothing you already own.

Review warehouse QC photos

When inspection photos are available, compare the color, labels, and visible condition with your selected variant. If fit is uncertain, ask whether a measurement photo is available before approving international shipping.

Estimate the delivered total

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any agent fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes. Compare shipping quotes using the destination and the packed parcel's weight and dimensions.

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

The catalog and both main buttons open HipobuyFind. This independent guide features its catalog alongside a checklist for people considering SuperBuy as their purchasing agent.

Can I use every listing with SuperBuy?

Check the original marketplace URL in SuperBuy. Support and availability can vary by listing, item type, and destination. Confirm your exact variant before paying.

What does the USD reference price include?

It represents the catalog's approximate conversion of the listed item price. Check agent fees, delivery charges, and taxes separately to estimate your total.

Where should I check QC photos?

Check your agent's warehouse order record when the item arrives. Match the photos to your order and selected variant before authorizing shipment.
